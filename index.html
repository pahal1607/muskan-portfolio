01 / A little about me

Spaces that
feel like home.

I'm Muskan Pandit, an interior design student with a strong interest in residential space planning, visualisation and material selection. I enjoy bringing function and aesthetics together to create considered, liveable spaces.

My process combines practical planning with an eye for atmosphere — from early layouts and moodboards to detailed drawings, lighting and 3D views.
